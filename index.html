    Headmistress' Welcome

    It is with great pride and joy that I welcome you to the official website of Ahantaman Girls' Senior High School — a distinguished institution dedicated to empowering young women through quality education, discipline, and character development. At Ahantaman Girls, we are committed to nurturing confident, visionary, and morally upright individuals who are prepared to make meaningful contributions to society. Our school has built a solid reputation for academic excellence and holistic growth, thanks to the dedication of our hardworking staff, the determination of our students, and the unwavering support of parents and alumni. This website offers a glimpse into our vibrant school life, our values, programs, and achievements. Whether you are a prospective student, a parent, an old girl, or a visitor, we invite you to explore and discover what makes Ahantaman Girls truly exceptional.

    Our History

    Ahantaman Girls’ Senior High School was established in 1948 as a private commercial school with 5 students. In 1965, the Ghana Education Service absorbed the school into the secondary school system.
